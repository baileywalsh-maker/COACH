Food data

    

      
USDA nutrient top-ups
    

      COACH tracks 36 nutrients. Most foods only carry a handful, because that is all a
      label declares. A free USDA FoodData Central key adds about 20,000 whole foods with a
      full laboratory profile. It also fills in every built-in food in one go, and tops up
      anything new you log from then on without being asked.

    
    

      
      
    

    

    
    
    

    
    

      Your key stays on this device and is only ever sent to USDA. Nothing already saved is
      touched: downloaded figures fill blanks and never overwrite a number COACH already had.

Calorie cycling
